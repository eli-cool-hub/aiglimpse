About AI Glimpse

What we cover

AI Glimpse is an independent publication covering artificial intelligence: the people, products, research, and policies shaping the field. We publish daily news, in-depth analysis, and explainers across seven beats, LLMs & Chatbots, AI Research, Tools & Products, Business, Ethics & Policy, Industry Applications, and Robotics.

Masthead

AI Glimpse is published by the AI Glimpse Newsroom, an independent editorial desk. Day-to-day coverage is produced by our newsroom editors with AI-assisted drafting under human review. We do not publish under personal bylines for automated news briefs; evergreen explainers are attributed to the AI Glimpse Newsroom and dated with a last-reviewed stamp when updated.

Corrections and standards questions: corrections@aiglimpse.ai. Tips: tips@aiglimpse.ai.

Editorial independence

We don't accept payment for editorial coverage. Sponsored content, when it appears, is clearly labeled and produced separately from our newsroom. Our revenue comes from reader subscriptions and, when live, advertising that is disclosed in our Privacy Policy.

How we work with AI

We use AI tools to monitor news sources, summarize source material, and assist with drafting. Every published article is reviewed for accuracy and editorial quality before publication. We disclose AI-assisted processes on our Editorial Standards page and never use generative tools to fabricate quotes, statistics, or sources.
