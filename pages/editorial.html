Editorial Standards

Our commitments

We report accurately, attribute sources clearly, correct mistakes quickly, and disclose conflicts of interest. We are independent of any AI lab, investor, or platform we cover.

Sourcing

We prefer named, on-the-record sources. Anonymous sources are used only when warranted and when their information can be independently verified. We do not publish unverified leaks.

AI-assisted reporting

Our automated news pipeline aggregates from licensed news APIs and reputable public sources. AI tools assist with summarization and drafting, but every article is reviewed before publication. We never use AI to fabricate quotes, sources, or data.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please email corrections@aiglimpse.ai. We log all corrections on the affected article and date them clearly.
