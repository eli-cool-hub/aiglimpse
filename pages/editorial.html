Editorial Standards

Our commitments

We report accurately, attribute sources clearly, correct mistakes quickly, and disclose conflicts of interest. We are independent of any AI lab, investor, or platform we cover.

Sourcing

We prefer named, on-the-record sources. Anonymous sources are used only when warranted and when their information can be independently verified. We do not publish unverified leaks. News briefs cite the primary outlet in a "Based on reporting from…" footer with a link.

AI-assisted reporting

Our automated news pipeline aggregates from licensed news APIs and reputable public sources (lab blogs, arXiv, community signals). AI tools assist with summarization and drafting, but every article is reviewed before publication. We never use AI to fabricate quotes, sources, or data.

Corrections policy

If you spot an error, email corrections@aiglimpse.ai. We investigate promptly. When we correct a factual error, we:

  • Update the article body as soon as the fix is verified
  • Add a dated corrections note at the bottom of the article describing what changed
  • Keep the original publication date and update dateModified in structured data when the fix is material

Minor typos that do not change meaning may be fixed silently. Significant corrections are always disclosed on the page.

Reader expectations

News briefs are timely summaries of public reporting and research. Evergreen explainers are maintained guides: we revise them as the field changes and note major updates.
