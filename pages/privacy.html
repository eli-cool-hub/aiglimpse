Privacy Policy

Information we collect

We collect minimal information: email addresses you provide to subscribe to our newsletter, and anonymized analytics data via Google Analytics about how visitors use the site.

We use cookies for analytics and to serve advertisements via Google AdSense. You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ad Settings.
