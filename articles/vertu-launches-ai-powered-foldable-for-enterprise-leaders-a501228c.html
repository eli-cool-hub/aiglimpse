Vertu Launches AI-Powered Foldable for Enterprise Leaders

Luxury device maker enters competitive AI hardware market with executive-focused foldable starting at $6,880, built on open-source Hermes foundation.

By AI Glimpse Newsroom
·
May 28, 2026

Vertu, the high-end smartphone manufacturer known for catering to affluent professionals, has unveiled an ambitious new product that combines artificial intelligence capabilities with premium mobile hardware. The company's latest foldable device represents a deliberate pivot toward the enterprise market, specifically targeting chief executives and C-suite executives who demand both computational power and luxury aesthetics.

According to TechCrunch AI, the device leverages the open-source Hermes project as its technological foundation, enabling sophisticated AI-agent workflows that can automate complex business processes directly from the device. This architecture allows executives to delegate routine operational tasks to AI systems while maintaining oversight and control from a single interface.

Enterprise Integration at the Core

The foldable's value proposition centers on seamless integration with existing corporate infrastructure. Rather than treating AI as an isolated feature, Vertu has positioned the device as a unified command center for business operations. Key capabilities include:

  • AI-powered agent systems capable of executing multi-step workflows without manual intervention
  • Direct integration with enterprise software ecosystems and backend systems
  • Ultra-premium build quality and materials reflecting Vertu's luxury positioning
  • Competitive pricing that undercuts comparable enterprise devices while maintaining margin expectations

The starting price of $6,880 positions the device at a premium relative to mainstream foldables, yet within reach of corporate technology budgets allocated for executive mobility tools. This pricing strategy acknowledges that business users prioritize capability and reliability over mass-market affordability.

Hermes Foundation and Open Architecture

Building on Hermes, an open-source framework, provides Vertu with significant advantages. The foundation grants access to a growing ecosystem of developers and enterprises already familiar with the platform. This approach reduces development risk while positioning the device within an established technological community.

The open-source nature also signals Vertu's confidence in competing on integration and polish rather than proprietary lock-in alone. Enterprises increasingly value interoperability, and Vertu's choice to embrace Hermes reflects that market reality.

Targeting a Specific Market Segment

While consumer foldables compete primarily on battery life and durability, Vertu's approach emphasizes productivity acceleration and operational efficiency. The implicit argument is that executives who delegate significant responsibilities through AI systems can reclaim hours of daily time previously consumed by oversight tasks.

This represents a calculated departure from Vertu's traditional strength in delivering status symbols. The new device asks executives to justify purchases on operational grounds while maintaining the prestige historically associated with the brand.

The competitive landscape has shifted considerably since Vertu's earlier ventures into luxury smartphones. Samsung, Apple, and emerging AI-native companies all recognize that foldables represent a growing segment. Vertu's entry signals that even established luxury brands see the fold-factor as essential to next-generation mobile computing.

Whether the enterprise market will embrace a luxury foldable as a core business tool remains uncertain. However, Vertu's decision to commit engineering resources and brand capital to this segment underscores genuine conviction that AI-enhanced devices represent the future of executive mobility.

Based on reporting from TechCrunch AI.
