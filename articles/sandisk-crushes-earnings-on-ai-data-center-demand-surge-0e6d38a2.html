SanDisk Crushes Earnings on AI Data Center Demand Surge

Memory maker reports record quarterly revenue driven by explosive datacenter storage needs, signaling robust enterprise AI infrastructure buildout.

By AI Glimpse Newsroom
·
August 5, 2026

Storage specialist SanDisk delivered blockbuster financial results in its fiscal fourth quarter, underscoring the intensity of demand for high-capacity memory infrastructure supporting artificial intelligence workloads across the enterprise sector. According to AI Weekly, the company posted quarterly revenue of $8.97 billion, representing a 51% increase from the prior quarter and a staggering 372% surge compared to the year-ago period.

The earnings report illuminates a critical inflection point in the AI hardware supply chain. Datacenter revenue alone reached $2.98 billion, effectively doubling sequentially as organizations race to provision storage systems capable of handling the massive data requirements inherent to large language models and generative AI applications. This segment now represents approximately one-third of the company's total quarterly revenue.

Profitability and Capital Returns Signal Confidence

Beyond top-line growth, SanDisk demonstrated exceptional profitability metrics that underscore the company's pricing power in a supply-constrained environment. The company achieved an adjusted gross margin of 84.6%, a figure reflecting strong demand dynamics and limited competitive pressure. The company reported GAAP diluted earnings per share of $43.97 and non-GAAP EPS of $39.25 for the quarter.

Management's confidence in future performance extended to capital allocation decisions. SanDisk authorized an additional $14 billion in share repurchases, bringing total remaining authorization to $15 billion. The aggressive buyback program signals leadership's conviction that current valuations do not fully reflect the company's position within the expanding AI infrastructure market.

Market Context and Competitive Implications

The exceptional results arrive amid heightened competition for enterprise AI spending. Data center operators, cloud providers, and AI systems integrators are competing intensely to acquire sufficient storage capacity to support model training and inference operations. SanDisk's ability to capture such substantial revenue growth suggests the company has effectively positioned itself to capitalize on this secular trend.

  • Sequential revenue growth of 51% indicates accelerating demand momentum
  • Year-over-year growth of 372% demonstrates the magnitude of AI-driven infrastructure expansion
  • Gross margins near 85% provide substantial reinvestment capacity for product development
  • Datacenter segment growth outpacing overall company growth highlights AI as primary growth driver

Looking Ahead

The fiscal Q4 performance establishes a baseline for evaluating near-term enterprise technology spending patterns. If SanDisk's sequential acceleration continues, it would provide compelling evidence that organizations have moved beyond pilot programs and proof-of-concept deployments into sustained, large-scale AI infrastructure buildout. Conversely, any moderation in datacenter growth could signal either supply chain normalization or slowing enterprise AI adoption rates.

The results position SanDisk as a critical beneficiary of the infrastructure phase of the AI boom, one that may serve as a more reliable indicator of genuine enterprise AI deployment than hardware supplier guidance or venture capital funding announcements.

Based on reporting from AI Weekly.
