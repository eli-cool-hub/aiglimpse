NTT DATA Slashes Incident Response Time to 30 Minutes Using AI

The Japanese IT giant demonstrates how enterprise AI platforms can dramatically accelerate operational workflows across thousands of workers.

By AI Glimpse Newsroom
·
July 22, 2026

NTT DATA Group, one of the world's largest IT services providers, has achieved significant operational gains by deploying advanced AI tools across its workforce. The company reports cutting incident analysis time from hours to just 30 minutes, marking a substantial productivity improvement for enterprise operations that typically handle thousands of concurrent systems.

According to OpenAI, NTT DATA has integrated ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex, OpenAI's code generation model, to automate routine tasks across approximately 9,000 employees. The initiative represents a broader shift in how large organizations are approaching digital transformation, moving beyond isolated pilot projects to company-wide AI adoption at scale.

Accelerating Incident Management

Incident response represents one of the highest-friction points in IT operations. When systems fail, the initial analysis phase often consumes the most time as teams manually investigate logs, correlate data points, and identify root causes. By leveraging AI models trained on vast technical documentation and code repositories, NTT DATA has automated much of this diagnostic work.

The 30-minute threshold is significant because it keeps incident response within the critical window where escalation and remediation become exponentially more expensive. Faster analysis translates directly to reduced downtime, which particularly matters for enterprise customers operating mission-critical infrastructure.

Enterprise AI Strategy

NTT DATA's approach highlights how organizations are moving beyond ChatGPT's consumer interface toward purpose-built enterprise deployments. ChatGPT Enterprise offers enhanced security, longer context windows, and faster processing speeds compared to standard subscriptions, making it suitable for handling sensitive customer data and complex technical documentation.

The integration with Codex enables employees to:

  • Generate and review code suggestions with AI assistance
  • Automate script writing for repetitive infrastructure tasks
  • Reduce manual debugging through AI-powered code analysis
  • Accelerate documentation and knowledge base creation

Scaling Secure Adoption

A critical challenge for large enterprises deploying AI involves security and compliance. NTT DATA's implementation underscores how organizations are addressing these concerns through controlled rollout strategies and enterprise-grade infrastructure. The company has ensured that AI tools integrate with existing governance frameworks while maintaining data privacy across different business units.

This approach matters because many enterprises remain hesitant about AI adoption due to regulatory concerns, data residency requirements, and vendor lock-in risks. NTT DATA's success suggests that thoughtful deployment architecture can overcome these barriers while delivering measurable business value.

Broader Implications for Enterprise AI

The results indicate that AI tools are moving beyond experimental phases into operational production environments. When organizations like NTT DATA, which manages complex infrastructure for thousands of enterprise clients, embed AI into core workflows, it signals market maturation and real-world viability.

The 9,000-employee deployment also demonstrates that AI adoption is no longer limited to specialized technical teams. Broader workforce engagement with AI tools suggests that organizations expect productivity gains across multiple functions, from infrastructure management to business operations.

As AI tools become embedded in enterprise operations, the competitive advantage will likely shift from simply deploying the technology to optimizing how organizations integrate AI with existing processes while maintaining security, compliance, and user adoption.

Based on reporting from OpenAI.
