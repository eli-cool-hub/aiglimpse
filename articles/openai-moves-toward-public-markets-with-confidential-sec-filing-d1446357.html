OpenAI Moves Toward Public Markets With Confidential SEC Filing

The AI leader joins Anthropic in pursuing IPO registration, escalating competition between the industry's two most prominent startups.

By AI Glimpse Newsroom
·
June 8, 2026

OpenAI has taken a significant step toward becoming a publicly traded company, submitting a confidential registration statement to the Securities and Exchange Commission on Monday. The move marks a critical milestone in the ongoing race between two of artificial intelligence's most influential organizations to access public capital markets.

According to The Verge AI, the confidential filing follows Anthropic's similar submission on June 1st. Both companies have been competing aggressively over the past year to pursue initial public offerings, and OpenAI's recent action demonstrates the acceleration of that competition.

What Confidential Filing Means

The confidential nature of OpenAI's Form S-1 submission means the public does not yet have access to detailed operational and financial information that will eventually become part of the official registration statement. Key data points remaining under wraps include executive compensation packages, detailed financial performance metrics, and comprehensive risk assessments for the company's business model.

This confidential phase allows OpenAI to work directly with SEC regulators to address potential compliance issues and refine disclosures before making them public. The process typically takes several months before a company moves to public filing status.

The Stakes in AI's Public Market Race

Both OpenAI and Anthropic are seeking to monetize their significant investor backing and technological achievements at a moment when artificial intelligence companies command extraordinary valuations. Anthropic holds the distinction of being valued as the world's most valuable startup following its recent fundraising round.

  • OpenAI has raised multiple funding rounds totaling tens of billions of dollars from major institutional investors
  • Anthropic has similarly attracted billions in venture capital from prominent technology firms and strategic partners
  • Both companies are developing large language models that power commercial applications across industries

The Broader IPO Context

The confidential filings represent a shift in how artificial intelligence companies are approaching capital markets. Rather than pursuing traditional venture funding indefinitely, both organizations have concluded that public markets offer a more sustainable path for funding ongoing research and scaling operations.

The timing reflects market confidence in AI commercialization, despite ongoing debates about regulation, safety, and societal impact. Public markets would give both companies access to deeper capital pools while subjecting them to increased transparency requirements and shareholder oversight.

OpenAI's filing underscores its position as the commercial frontrunner in the AI sector, given the widespread adoption of its ChatGPT platform and enterprise products. However, Anthropic's earlier move to initiate the IPO process signals determination to compete aggressively for public market prominence as well.

The coming months will reveal details about both companies' financial performance, growth trajectories, and business risks as they progress through the regulatory approval process. These disclosures will provide the first comprehensive public view into how the leading AI organizations are generating revenue and managing their technology development at scale.
