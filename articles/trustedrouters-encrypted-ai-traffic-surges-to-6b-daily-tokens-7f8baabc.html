TrustedRouter's Encrypted AI Traffic Surges to 6B Daily Tokens

Miami startup demonstrates six-fold growth in privacy-focused AI processing, raising questions about enterprise adoption of encrypted LLM infrastructure.

By AI Glimpse Newsroom
·
November 26, 2026

A Miami-based startup has achieved a significant milestone in encrypted artificial intelligence infrastructure, handling more than 6 billion language model tokens daily across enterprise customers. According to AI Weekly, TrustedRouter's token volume has increased six-fold since August, when the company processed approximately 1 billion tokens per day.

The company's core value proposition centers on a technical approach that keeps both the startup and its data center partners blind to customer prompts and model outputs. This design ensures that neither TrustedRouter nor the infrastructure providers hosting the AI models can access or observe the sensitive information flowing through their systems.

Founder and Chief Executive Joseph Perla emphasized the nascent nature of this privacy approach in conversations with industry observers. "Almost nobody knows that you can actually encrypt the AI chats," he said, suggesting that many enterprises remain unaware of encryption capabilities available for large language model interactions. His explanation of the company's mission reduces to a fundamental principle: treating security not as an afterthought but as an essential baseline requirement for all operations.

Market Gap and Enterprise Demand

The rapid scaling from 1 billion to 6 billion daily tokens indicates substantial enterprise demand for privacy-preserving AI infrastructure. This growth trajectory suggests that organizations increasingly view data protection in AI workflows as non-negotiable, particularly given regulatory scrutiny around sensitive information processing.

TrustedRouter's model addresses a specific vulnerability in current AI deployment patterns. Many organizations hesitate to send confidential data, proprietary information, or regulated content to cloud-based language models due to concerns about data exposure or retention. By implementing encryption that shields data at every stage of processing, the startup removes a critical barrier to AI adoption in security-conscious industries.

Technical Architecture and Implications

The company's architecture relies on homomorphic encryption or similar cryptographic techniques that enable computation on encrypted data. This approach preserves functionality while maintaining confidentiality guarantees that extend across the entire inference pipeline.

  • Tokens processed daily increased from 1 billion in August to over 6 billion currently
  • Neither the startup nor hosting providers can access unencrypted customer data
  • Architecture treats security as foundational rather than optional
  • Model targets enterprises with sensitive data or regulatory compliance requirements

The technical achievement carries broader implications for AI industry standards. If encryption-first infrastructure becomes commonplace, it could reshape how enterprises evaluate AI platform providers, with security guarantees becoming a primary selection criterion rather than a differentiator.

TrustedRouter's growth also highlights a potential gap in existing AI infrastructure offerings from major cloud providers. While Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud have expanded privacy features, startups like TrustedRouter can focus exclusively on maximizing encryption guarantees and minimizing access to customer information across the model serving layer.

Looking Forward

As enterprise AI adoption accelerates, the demand for privacy-preserving infrastructure will likely intensify. TrustedRouter's six-fold token volume increase in three months suggests that organizations in regulated industries such as finance, healthcare, and legal services have already begun shifting workloads to platforms offering stronger confidentiality guarantees.

The company's trajectory may also influence how founders conceptualize AI infrastructure challenges. Rather than viewing privacy as a compliance checkbox, TrustedRouter demonstrates that treating encryption as a core technical requirement can serve as a compelling market differentiator.
