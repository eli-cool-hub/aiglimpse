Musician Lorde Critiques AI Glasses at Madrid Festival Performance

The artist's on-stage remarks highlight growing cultural skepticism toward AI-powered wearables and their integration into everyday life.

By AI Glimpse Newsroom
·
July 12, 2026

During a performance at the Real Cool Festival in Madrid on Thursday, musician Lorde used her platform to voice concerns about artificial intelligence integrated into consumer eyewear products. According to The Verge, the artist made pointed comments about AI-enabled smartglasses while addressing the audience between songs.

Though Lorde stopped short of naming specific brands, her timing and context suggest she was directing criticism toward Ray-Ban's Meta collaboration. The eyewear brand served as a sponsor of the Spanish music festival and produces a line of AI smartglasses developed in partnership with Meta.

What She Said

After expressing gratitude to attendees for participating in "something real," Lorde reflected on the increasingly blurred boundaries between authentic and artificial experiences. She articulated frustration with the ambiguity created by wearable AI devices, questioning whether someone is wearing ordinary sunglasses or AI-equipped smartglasses capable of capturing video and leveraging artificial intelligence to interpret their surroundings.

The comments were captured in multiple videos shared across social media platforms, amplifying her message beyond the concert venue.

Broader Context for AI Wearables

Lorde's criticism reflects wider cultural tension surrounding AI-powered consumer devices. Smart eyewear represents one of the most visible and intimate forms of AI integration, as these devices sit on the wearer's face and can continuously record video and audio from the wearer's perspective.

  • Privacy concerns persist around recording capabilities and data collection
  • Consumers struggle to visually distinguish AI-enabled glasses from regular eyewear
  • Questions linger about informed consent when others don't know they might be recorded
  • Cultural skepticism grows regarding constant AI surveillance integration

Celebrity Perspectives Shape Technology Discourse

High-profile artists and public figures increasingly serve as barometers for mainstream attitudes toward emerging technology. When musicians, actors, and other cultural influencers express reservations about AI products, their reach extends discussions beyond technology circles into broader pop culture conversations.

Lorde's stage remarks demonstrate how consumer skepticism about artificial intelligence and its applications continues gaining momentum. Rather than focusing on technical specifications or capabilities, her critique centered on cultural and aesthetic dimensions: the inability to distinguish AI-enabled devices from conventional products, and the discomfort this creates around consent and authenticity.

The Ray-Ban Meta Partnership

Ray-Ban and Meta's smartglasses line incorporates artificial intelligence features including real-time object recognition, translation capabilities, and scene understanding. The devices represent Meta's broader push to establish itself as a central player in AI-powered hardware alongside its software and research initiatives.

As AI wearables move from niche products toward mainstream consumer markets, reception from cultural arbiters like Lorde may influence how broadly these devices are adopted and which design or privacy features manufacturers prioritize in future iterations.
