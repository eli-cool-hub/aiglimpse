DOJ Scrutinizes Nvidia's Hugging Face Acquisition Over AI Market Control

Federal antitrust investigators are examining whether the chipmaker's proposed purchase could consolidate control over critical AI infrastructure.

By AI Glimpse Newsroom
·
October 28, 2026

The Justice Department is conducting an active inquiry into Nvidia's proposed $12.93 billion acquisition of Hugging Face, raising concerns that the transaction could entrench the computing giant's influence over essential AI ecosystem layers. According to AI Weekly, antitrust officials are investigating whether the deal would enable Nvidia to dominate what regulators view as a crucial checkpoint for distributing artificial intelligence models to the broader market.

The examination represents a continuation of a multi-year antitrust investigation into Nvidia's market practices that began during the Biden administration and has persisted into the current regulatory environment. Hugging Face operates one of the most widely used repositories for open-source machine learning models, hosting thousands of pre-trained systems that developers and researchers rely on for building AI applications.

Market Control Concerns

Regulators appear focused on whether Nvidia's control of both the hardware infrastructure underlying AI systems and the distribution mechanisms for models could create an anti-competitive advantage. The investigation reflects broader scrutiny of how Nvidia's dominant position in AI chips might extend into related markets as the technology sector consolidates around large language model infrastructure.

Hugging Face's platform has become central to the AI development workflow, functioning as a standardized location where machine learning practitioners discover, download, and deploy models. By acquiring this distribution channel alongside its existing semiconductor dominance, critics worry Nvidia could disadvantage competitors or extract terms favorable to its own interests.

Regulatory Landscape Shift

The timing of this probe reflects evolving antitrust enforcement priorities. While previous scrutiny of Nvidia centered on its graphics processing unit market leadership, this inquiry extends into how technology companies can leverage market power across vertically integrated business lines. The investigation underscores how artificial intelligence's rapid commercialization has created new competitive flashpoints for regulators assessing fair market dynamics.

  • DOJ examining potential control over AI model distribution
  • Probe continues Biden-era antitrust investigation trajectory
  • Hugging Face serves as critical infrastructure for ML development
  • Concerns focus on vertical integration across hardware and software layers

The inquiry does not indicate that regulators will necessarily block the transaction, but signals they intend to carefully evaluate the competitive implications before the deal concludes. Nvidia and Hugging Face have not publicly commented on the investigation's scope or timeline.

This development illustrates how the artificial intelligence industry's infrastructure consolidation is encountering intensified regulatory review. As major technology companies position themselves across multiple layers of AI development and deployment, antitrust authorities worldwide are reassessing what constitutes problematic market concentration in this rapidly evolving sector.

Based on reporting from AI Weekly.
