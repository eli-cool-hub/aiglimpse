State Officials Launch Broad Inquiry Into OpenAI Practices

Multiple state attorneys general are examining the AI company's advertising and medical data policies, marking a significant escalation in regulatory scrutiny.

By AI Glimpse Newsroom
·
June 13, 2026

OpenAI is facing coordinated scrutiny from multiple state law enforcement agencies investigating the company's business practices, according to TechCrunch AI. The investigation represents one of the most serious regulatory challenges the artificial intelligence company has encountered since achieving mainstream prominence.

While the specific states involved have not been publicly identified, the scope of the inquiry is notably expansive. Prosecutors are examining how OpenAI manages its advertising policies and processes sensitive medical information provided by users. The breadth of this investigation suggests regulators are concerned about how the company handles consumer data and the accuracy of promotional claims.

Regulatory Expansion in AI Oversight

The state-level investigation comes amid growing pressure on major AI companies to face stricter accountability measures. Unlike federal oversight, which has remained relatively limited, state attorneys general have demonstrated willingness to aggressively pursue technology firms on consumer protection grounds.

The focus on health data handling is particularly significant given that OpenAI has expanded ChatGPT's capabilities to address medical queries and provide health-related information. Such functionality creates potential exposure under state consumer protection laws if the system makes false or misleading health claims, or if the company inadequately safeguards sensitive personal health information.

Key Areas of Investigation

  • Advertising policies and promotional claims made by the company
  • Protocols for collecting, storing, and protecting health-related personal data
  • Compliance with state consumer protection statutes
  • Practices governing how user information is processed and retained

The investigation also points to broader questions about how AI systems should be regulated when they provide information on sensitive subjects like health and medicine. Unlike traditional medical advice providers, AI chatbots operate in a regulatory gray area where liability is unclear.

What This Means for the AI Industry

If multiple states are indeed coordinating on this matter, it could establish precedent for how other AI companies face scrutiny. State attorneys general have historically served as laboratories for regulatory innovation, with successful enforcement actions often prompting similar investigations elsewhere.

The timing is noteworthy as the artificial intelligence sector continues scaling rapidly without comprehensive federal privacy or safety regulations. State officials may be stepping into this void to protect constituents from potential harms related to AI system deployment.

"According to TechCrunch AI, prosecutors are examining everything from OpenAI's advertising policies to its handling of health data."

OpenAI has not publicly commented on the investigation. The company has previously stated its commitment to responsible AI development and user privacy protections, though these investigations suggest that state regulators may have concerns about whether current practices meet legal standards.

This development reflects a turning point in AI regulation, with state governments increasingly willing to independently challenge major AI companies rather than waiting for federal action. As the investigation unfolds, its outcomes could significantly shape how artificial intelligence companies manage their services and user data going forward.
