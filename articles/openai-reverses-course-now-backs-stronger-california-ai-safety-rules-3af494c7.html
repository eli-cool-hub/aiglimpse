OpenAI Reverses Course, Now Backs Stronger California AI Safety Rules

The AI leader previously opposed SB 53 but now advocates for enhanced safeguards, signaling a shift in industry positions on state-level regulation.

By AI Glimpse Newsroom
·
August 22, 2026

OpenAI has reversed its stance on California's proposed artificial intelligence oversight legislation, now endorsing a more stringent version of SB 53 after initially resisting the measure. The about-face reflects evolving corporate attitudes toward state-level AI governance as lawmakers craft regulatory frameworks for the rapidly advancing technology sector.

According to TechCrunch AI, the San Francisco-based company that created ChatGPT has called on California legislators to strengthen specific provisions within the safety bill. This represents a notable departure from OpenAI's earlier position, when the organization had publicly opposed the legislation as initially drafted.

Strategic Shift in AI Regulation Stance

The change in OpenAI's position underscores the complexity of regulating artificial intelligence at the state level. Companies previously wary of strict oversight requirements are now seeing potential benefits in well-designed regulatory frameworks, particularly those that establish clear baseline standards across jurisdictions.

OpenAI's support for enhanced safeguards aligns with broader industry recognition that some form of governance is likely inevitable. By advocating for stronger protections now, the company may be attempting to shape standards in ways that it believes are technically feasible and economically sustainable.

What SB 53 Addresses

  • Safety testing and evaluation requirements for advanced AI systems
  • Incident reporting and disclosure obligations for developers
  • Compliance mechanisms for large language models and other AI applications
  • Potential audit and oversight procedures

The bill has emerged as a significant test case for how states might regulate generative AI and other emerging technologies. California's legislative efforts carry particular weight given the state's outsized influence on technology policy and its role as home to many AI research and development centers.

Industry Convergence on AI Governance

OpenAI's endorsement of stronger protections suggests the AI industry may be moving toward consensus that some regulatory structure serves long-term interests better than unrestrained development. Other major AI companies and research organizations have similarly acknowledged the need for safety-focused oversight mechanisms.

The shift also reflects lessons learned from AI deployment in real-world contexts. Issues ranging from algorithmic bias to model reliability have demonstrated that robust safety practices benefit both creators and users of AI systems.

OpenAI's move to support enhanced safeguards represents a pragmatic acknowledgment that proactive regulation may prevent more restrictive measures down the line.

The company's specific recommendations for strengthening SB 53 remain partly unclear, though industry observers expect proposals around clearer technical standards, improved transparency requirements, and more rigorous testing protocols for high-stakes applications.

California's approach to AI regulation could set a precedent for federal legislation and other state initiatives. How lawmakers balance innovation encouragement with public protection will influence the regulatory landscape for years to come.
